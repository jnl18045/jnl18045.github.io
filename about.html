About Blind Date with a Book

Blind Date with a Book was created for readers who enjoy the excitement of discovering something new. Instead of judging a book only by its cover, our goal is to make choosing a book feel like a small adventure.

Our store focuses on making reading more fun by encouraging visitors to step outside their usual choices. Whether you already know what type of story you enjoy or want to try something completely different, Blind Date with a Book is designed to make discovering your next book exciting.
